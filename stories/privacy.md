*Last updated: October 26, 2026*

This is the personal website of Sef Kloninger, at <https://sef.kloninger.com/>.
The short version: I don't collect your personal information, and I don't track
you.

## What I collect

Nothing, directly. This site has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no comments, and
no advertising. It does not use analytics or tracking scripts, and it does not
set cookies of its own.

## Hosting

The site is a static website hosted on [GitHub Pages][pages]. Like any web
host, GitHub may log technical information about visits, such as IP addresses
and browser details, for security and operational purposes. I don't have access
to those logs. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement][ghprivacy] for details.

## Third-party content and links

Pages may link to other sites, such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Mastodon, and Bluesky,
or embed content from them. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and I
don't control what they collect. Following a link or loading embedded content
may send information to them.

## Email

If you email me, I'll see your email address and whatever you choose to send. I
use it only to read and reply to your message, and I don't share it.

## Children

This site is not directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect
information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date above.

Questions? Email me at [sefklon@gmail.com][email].
