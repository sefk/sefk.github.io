    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: October 26, 2026

    This is the personal website of Sef Kloninger, at https://sef.kloninger.com/. The short version: I don't collect your personal information, and I don't track you.

    What I collect

    Nothing, directly. This site has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no comments, and no advertising. It does not use analytics or tracking scripts, and it does not set cookies of its own.

    Sef Studio Desktop and Google user data

    Sef Studio Desktop is a personal application I wrote. If you authorize it, it uses Google's OAuth flow and the Gmail API to access your Google account data, such as email messages and metadata, only to provide the app's features to you.

    • Storage: data stays on your own computer. I don't send it to any server I operate or to anyone else.
    • Sharing: I don't sell, share, or transfer Google user data to third parties, and I don't use it for advertising.
    • Human access: nobody reads your data except as needed for security, to comply with law, or with your explicit consent.
    • Limited Use: the app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
    • Revoking access: you can revoke access at any time in your Google Account permissions, and you can delete any locally stored data by removing the app's data from your computer.

    Hosting

    The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information about visits, such as IP addresses and browser details, for security and operational purposes. I don't have access to those logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

    Third-party content and links

    Pages may link to other sites, such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Mastodon, and Bluesky, or embed content from them. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and I don't control what they collect. Following a link or loading embedded content may send information to them.

    Email

    If you email me, I'll see your email address and whatever you choose to send. I use it only to read and reply to your message, and I don't share it.

    Children

    This site is not directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

    Changes

    If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date above.
